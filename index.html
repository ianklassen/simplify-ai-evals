Simplify AI Evals

Low-risk work gets a model self-check and runs with a log. High-risk work stops for a human gate. The hard part is telling the two apart, before the model touches the work.

ianklassen/simplify-ai-evals The runnable example behind this demo, on GitHub.

The five-question risk test

Answer these for the action the output will cause, not for the draft itself. Drafting a refund email is harmless. Sending it is not.

  1. QUESTION 1

    Does this affect revenue?

  2. QUESTION 2

    Does it contain customer PII?

  3. QUESTION 3

    Is there a legal, regulatory, or compliance issue?

  4. QUESTION 4

    Could the customer experience be adversely affected (retention)?

  5. QUESTION 5

    Could the experience or process affect the company principles?

Any yes → HIGH risk All no → LOW risk
